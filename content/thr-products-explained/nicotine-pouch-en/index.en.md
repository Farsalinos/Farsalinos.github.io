**Nicotine pouches** are small pouches—smaller than a tea bag—placed under the upper lip for about **30 to 60 minutes**.

## What are the Key Characteristics & Ingredients?

- **Tobacco-Free:** This is their fundamental difference from traditional Swedish *snus*.
- **Composition:** They contain **plant fibers (cellulose), pharmaceutical-grade nicotine**, flavorings, sweeteners, glycerin, and potentially pH regulators.
- **Nicotine Content:** Ranges from **2 to 16 mg** per pouch, though products with much higher levels have appeared on the market.

## How did they Evolve Historically?

- **Ancestors:** Oral tobacco use spans centuries. Chewing tobacco, highly popular in America, spread to Europe around 1600. In Scandinavia, a variation mixed tobacco with salt and water for under-the-lip use. This wet snus became highly popular in the United States during the 19th century. However, it was impractical—it caused significant saliva production that couldn't be swallowed without nausea, forcing users to spit.
- **The 1970s:** Scientist Curt Enzell placed tobacco inside tea-bag pouches, tested them on himself, and finding they worked, paved the way for *Scandinavian Snus*.
- **The 2008 Breakthrough:** Karl Fagerström—creator of the Fagerström Test for Cigarette Dependence and pioneer of medical nicotine replacement therapy—developed the **first tobacco-free nicotine pouch**.

## What are the Main Differences from Cigarettes?

- **Slower Nicotine Absorption:** Unlike conventional cigarettes, where nicotine absorption is extremely rapid—reaching peak blood levels in about 5 to 6 minutes—nicotine pouches take five to ten times longer. Studies show maximum blood concentration is reached in **30 to 60 minutes**.
- **The Satisfaction Drawback:** This slower absorption is considered a disadvantage. The rapid, sharp spikes in blood nicotine levels satisfy smokers and provide smoking's greatest pleasure. Electronic cigarettes, by contrast, achieve nicotine delivery speed and quantity comparable to traditional cigarettes.

## How About Safety and Public Health?

- **High Safety Profile:** Among all tobacco harm reduction alternatives, nicotine pouches carry **the highest safety profile**. However, because they can still cause addiction, they are **not recommended for non-smokers**.
- **The "Swedish Success Story":** Sweden is the ultimate public health example—the only country in Europe (and likely the world) that is practically **smoke-free**, with a smoking rate **under 5%**. It achieved this milestone exclusively through widespread adoption of snus and nicotine pouches.
- **Different Delivery, Immense Benefit:** While Sweden's overall nicotine-use rate resembles the rest of Europe, Swedes consume it orally instead of burning and inhaling it. Consequently, Sweden records **by far the lowest rates of cardiovascular and cancer mortality** in Europe.
- **Combination Use for Cessation:** Many smokers find standard nicotine replacements insufficient to stop cravings. For these individuals, **combining electronic or heated cigarettes with nicotine pouches** can successfully suppress the urge to smoke.
