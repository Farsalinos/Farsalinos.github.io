**Nicotine pouches** are small pouches, smaller than the size of a tea bag, which are **placed under the upper lip** and remain stationary for about **30 to 60 minutes**.

**Key Characteristics & Ingredients**

- **Tobacco-Free:** This is their fundamental difference from traditional Swedish *snus*.

- **Composition:** They contain **plant fibers (cellulose), pharmaceutical-grade nicotine**, flavorings, sweeteners, glycerin, and potentially pH regulators.

- **Nicotine Content:** This ranges from **2 to 16 mg** per pouch, but products with much higher levels have been found in the market.

**Historical Evolution**

- **Ancestors:** The oral use of tobacco has a history spanning centuries. Chewing tobacco, which was highly popular in America, became popular in Europe around 1600. In Scandinavia, a variation was created by mixing tobacco with salt and water and placing it under the lip. This product, known as wet snus, became highly popular in the United States during the 19th century. However, it was impractical as it caused significant saliva production that could not be swallowed without causing nausea, forcing users to spit.

- **The 1970s:** Scientist Curt Enzell decided to place this tobacco inside pouches. He used tea bags, filled them with tobacco, and tested them on himself. Finding that it worked, he paved the way for the creation of *Scandinavian Snus*.

- **The 2008 Breakthrough:** The distinguished scientist Karl Fagerström—creator of the Fagerström Test for Cigarette Dependence and the pioneer behind medical nicotine replacement therapy—developed the **first tobacco-free nicotine pouch**.

**Nicotine Absorption & Difference from Cigarettes**

- **Slower Absorption:** In contrast to conventional cigarettes where nicotine absorption is extremely rapid—reaching peak blood levels in about 5 to 6 minutes—nicotine pouches take five to ten times longer. Studies show that their maximum blood concentration is achieved in **30 to 60 minutes**.

- **The Satisfaction Drawback:** This slower absorption rate is considered a disadvantage of nicotine pouches. The rapid, sharp spikes in blood nicotine levels are what satisfy a smoker and provide the greatest pleasure when smoking. On the other hand, electronic cigarettes achieve a speed and quantity of nicotine delivery comparable to traditional cigarettes.

**Safety and Public Health**

- **High Safety Profile:** Among all tobacco harm reduction alternatives, nicotine pouches carry **the highest safety profile**. However, because they can still cause addiction, they are **not recommended for non-smokers**.

- **The "Swedish Success Story":** Sweden represents the ultimate public health example, being the only country in Europe (and likely the world) that is practically **smoke-free**, with a smoking rate **under 5%**. It achieved this milestone exclusively through the widespread adoption of snus and nicotine pouches.

- **Different Delivery, Immense Benefit:** While the overall rate of nicotine use in Sweden is similar to the rest of Europe, Swedish citizens choose to consume it orally instead of burning and inhaling it. Consequently, Sweden records **by far the lowest rates of cardiovascular and cancer mortality** in Europe.

- **Combination Use for Cessation:** Many smokers find that standard nicotine replacements are insufficient to stop their cravings. For these individuals, **combining electronic or heated cigarettes with nicotine pouches** can successfully suppress the urge to smoke.
